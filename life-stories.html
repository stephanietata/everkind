1967 Age 26 domain Adelaide Bank, King William Street

First Desk on King William Street & Ledger Days

Margaret was one of four young women assigned to ledger reconciliations. She recalls the tram bell outside the stone bank portico, taking coffee at the original arcade café, and buying her first green tailored wool suit.

"We didn't have computers. We had handwriting, heavy leather binders, and absolute precision."
1972 Age 31 home Unley, South Australia

Wedding to Thomas & Laying the Red Bricks of Unley

Married on a brisk September afternoon. Thomas had spent nine months hand-sanding the cedar doorframes of their cottage. The garden began with two lemon trees that still bear fruit in the backyard fifty years later.

1985 Age 44

Raising Three Children & The Community Kitchen

David, Claire, and Simon filled the cottage with school sports, music rehearsals, and neighborhood dinners. Margaret initiated the Unley Community Kitchen garden, preserving fig preserves and heirloom tomato seeds.

2010 Age 69 navigation The Red Centre & Flinders Ranges

The Great Australian Caravan Road Trip

Eight weeks camping under the southern stars. From Wilpena Pound straight up to Uluru with Thomas in their beloved Toyota LandCruiser. Margaret kept an illustrated travel journal recording desert bird calls and campfire recipes.

Frequently Asked Questions by Families

What if Mum has mild cognitive changes or memory gaps?

Our companions are trained in compassionate reminiscence therapy techniques. We never quiz or test. We celebrate the sensory impressions, emotions, and treasured anecdotes that bring them joy.

Can family members contribute old photos or questions?

Absolutely. Families receive a dedicated portal to suggest favorite childhood themes, upload family photos, or submit gentle prompts for the companion to explore during morning tea.
