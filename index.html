Questions Answered

Frequently asked questions

Clear, straightforward answers without jargon or hidden commitments.

Is EverKind a clinical nursing or medical care provider? expand_more
No. EverKind intentionally focuses on pure social companionship, emotional connection, and everyday non-clinical assistance (such as walks, conversation, technology help, and errands). We do not administer medication, wound care, or hygiene bathing.
How are companions vetted and selected? expand_more
Every candidate undergoes Australian National Police vetting, Working with Vulnerable People checks, multiple direct phone reference checks, and an in-person behavioral interview. Fewer than 10% of applicants are selected to be EverKind companions.
How does pricing work? expand_more
We provide transparent hourly pricing starting from $48/hour with no lock-in contracts or exit fees. You can schedule one-off visits or weekly routines, pausing anytime your family's circumstances change.
Can we use Home Care Packages (HCP) or NDIS funding? expand_more
Yes, many of our clients fund EverKind companionship through self-managed or plan-managed Home Care Packages and NDIS funds under social participation categories. We provide clear itemized invoicing for package managers.
